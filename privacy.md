# Privacy Policy – Feedverse

Feedverse is a private content automation project intended for personal use.

## Google User Data

The application uses Google Drive and Rclone to access, upload and manage media files required for its content automation workflow.

Google user data is used solely to support this workflow. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

## Data Storage and Security

Media files are stored in the connected Google Drive account. Authentication credentials must be stored securely and must not be published in public repositories.

## Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner controls the retention and deletion of files and can revoke the application's access through their Google Account settings.
